Frequently asked questions

What is Albena?

Albena is a voice-first AI assistant for your home and life, built by Omar Huertas LLC. You say “Hey Albena” and she can help with your home, calendar, mail and everyday tasks. She is local-first and asks for your approval before she acts.

When will Albena be available?

Albena is in early access and not yet generally available. We are inviting people from the waitlist in small groups. There is no fixed public launch date yet; we will email waitlist members before anything changes for them.

Which devices does Albena run on?

Early access is focused on Apple devices, with a home hub that runs on a computer in your home. Support for more platforms and smart-home brands is planned. Tell us what you use when you join the waitlist and it helps us prioritize.

How does privacy work?

Albena processes as much as possible on hardware you control. This website sets no cookies and has no trackers, and we collect only the email address you give us for the waitlist. We never sell your data or use it for advertising. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Email us from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your data. We will remove your waitlist record and any early-access account data, and confirm when it is done. In the app you can also remove stored memories and disconnect calendar or mail at any time.

How do I contact you?

Email omar@dbaomarhuertasllc.com. For anything urgent about your account or data, put “Urgent” in the subject line.

Response times

We reply to support email within 2 business days, usually sooner. Data deletion and privacy requests are acknowledged within 5 business days and completed within 30 days. Albena is an early-access product built by a small team, so please bear with us during busy periods.

Not sure where to start? Just email us what you are trying to do. For legal documents, see the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.
