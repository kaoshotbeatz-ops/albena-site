Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Albena is meant for everyone in the household. We aim for this website to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

Conformance status

Our target is WCAG 2.2 level AA. We have reviewed every page of albena.ai against these criteria, but we have not yet had an independent audit, so we describe this as a target and not a certified result.

Measures we have taken

  • One main heading per page, a logical heading order and landmark regions (header, navigation, main, footer).
  • A “Skip to content” link on every page and full keyboard operation with a visible focus indicator.
  • Text and interface colors checked for at least 4.5:1 contrast against their backgrounds.
  • Labelled form fields and status messages announced to screen readers.
  • Decorative graphics hidden from assistive technology, and meaningful illustrations given text alternatives.
  • Interactive targets at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels, and layouts that reflow down to a 320 pixel width without horizontal scrolling.
  • Animation is switched off when your system requests reduced motion, and no content depends on hover.

Known limitations

  • The product illustration on the home page is a visual mock-up. Its content is summarized in a text description, but it is not interactive.
  • Albena’s voice-first experience is not yet available to preview here, so we have not yet assessed it with people who use assistive technology. Voice control is designed to complement, not replace, touch and keyboard options.
  • The waitlist form opens your email app to send your address, which depends on your device having a mail app configured. You can also email us directly.

Feedback and contact

If you hit a barrier on this website or want content in another format, email omar@dbaomarhuertasllc.com with the page and what went wrong. We aim to reply within 2 business days and to fix confirmed issues promptly.

Assessment approach

We tested with keyboard-only navigation, browser zoom and a 375 pixel wide viewport, automated checks and manual contrast review. This statement was prepared on October 9, 2026 and will be updated as the site and product change.
