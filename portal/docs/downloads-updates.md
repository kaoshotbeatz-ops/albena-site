# Downloads & Updates

## Update your Hub

Albena Hubs receive signed software updates automatically. Updates include bug fixes, security patches, and new features.

### Automatic updates

By default, your Hub checks for updates daily and installs them automatically during a low-activity window.

### Update channels

You can choose how quickly your Hub receives updates:

**Stable**  
Recommended for most users. Updates are tested thoroughly before release.

**Beta**  
Get new features early, but updates may be less stable. Only use if you want to help test upcoming releases.

To change your update channel:

1. Go to [account.albena.ai](https://account.albena.ai)
2. Click your Hub
3. Click **Settings** > **Updates**
4. Select **Stable** or **Beta**

### Automatic rollback

If an update causes problems, your Hub automatically rolls back to the previous version. You'll see a notification.

## Download Albena software

To install Albena on your own hardware (Bring Your Own plan):

1. Go to [albena.ai/download](https://albena.ai/download)
2. Choose your operating system
3. Follow the installation instructions

All downloads are signed and verified for security.
