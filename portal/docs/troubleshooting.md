# Troubleshooting

## Can't sign in

**Pairing code expired**  
If your pairing code expired (more than 10 minutes), restart your Hub's setup to generate a new code, then try pairing again.

**Passkey not working**  
Try signing in from a different device, or add a new passkey:

1. Click **Trouble signing in?** on the sign-in page
2. Use your email to verify your identity
3. Add a new passkey once you're signed in

**Forgot your email**  
Contact [albena.ai/support](https://albena.ai/support) with details about your account.

## Hub not connecting

**Pairing failed**  
- Make sure the 8-character code is correct (case-insensitive)
- The code expires in 10 minutes — if it's expired, restart your Hub's setup
- Check that your Hub is powered on and connected to the internet

**Hub appears offline**  
- Restart your Hub
- Check that your internet connection is stable
- If your Hub is far from your router, move it closer or add a network extender
- Check the Hub's status light — if it's red, restart the device

## Can't update my Hub

**Update stuck**  
- Restart your Hub
- Check that your internet connection is stable
- Go to **Settings** > **Updates** and check your update channel

**Update failed, trying rollback**  
Your Hub automatically rolls back if an update fails. Wait a few minutes, then restart your Hub.

## Billing issues

**Payment declined**  
- Check that your card details are correct in your billing settings
- Try a different card
- Contact your bank to see if there's an issue

**Can't access billing portal**  
- Make sure you're signed in to [account.albena.ai](https://account.albena.ai)
- Click **Settings** > **Billing** > **Manage billing**
- Clear your browser's cache and try again

## Account access

**Locked out of my account**  
- Click **Trouble signing in?** on the sign-in page
- Verify your email address
- Follow the recovery steps

**Want to remove a household member**  
- Go to **Settings** > **Household**
- Find the member and click **Remove**
- They'll lose access to your Hub and account

## Still need help?

If your issue isn't listed here, visit [albena.ai/support](https://albena.ai/support) to contact our support team. Include:

- What you were trying to do
- What happened instead
- Any error messages you saw
- Your Hub model (if applicable)
