# Orders & Shipping

## Track your order

When you order an Albena Hub, you'll receive a confirmation email with tracking information.

1. Go to [account.albena.ai](https://account.albena.ai)
2. Click **Orders** to view your order status and tracking details
3. Click an order to see more details

## Warranty & returns

Detailed warranty coverage, return policies, and shipping information are available at [albena.ai/orders](https://albena.ai/orders).

For questions about your specific order, contact [albena.ai/support](https://albena.ai/support).

---

**Trouble with your order?** Visit [albena.ai/support](https://albena.ai/support) for assistance.
