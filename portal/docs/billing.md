# Billing

## Plans

Albena offers four subscription options:

**Bring Your Own**  
Run Albena on your own hardware (Mac, Linux, or other compatible devices). Ideal for users with existing systems.

**Hub for Mac**  
Everything you need in a Mac mini. Includes Albena software and hardware, pre-configured and ready to go.

**Hub for NVIDIA**  
Albena Hub built on NVIDIA hardware. Designed for more demanding setups.

**Estate**  
Full family and home setup. For multiple users, multiple rooms, and advanced automation.

All plans billed monthly or annually. Pricing is announced at launch — check [albena.ai](https://albena.ai) for details.

## Manage your billing

1. Go to [account.albena.ai](https://account.albena.ai)
2. Click **Settings** > **Billing**
3. Click **Manage billing** to access your billing portal

In your billing portal, you can:
- Update your payment method
- View invoices
- Download receipts
- Cancel your subscription at any time

## One subscription at a time

An account can have one open checkout and one subscription at a time. If you start a different plan before paying, the earlier checkout is closed. If a subscription is overdue or still being set up, you cannot start another; fix or cancel the existing one in your billing portal first. Your plan and billing interval always follow the plan you are currently subscribed to in Stripe, including changes made in the billing portal.

## Payments

We use Stripe to process payments securely. You can pay by credit card or other methods Stripe supports. We never store your card data directly.

## Billing questions

For questions about your plan, invoice, or payment, visit [albena.ai/support](https://albena.ai/support).
