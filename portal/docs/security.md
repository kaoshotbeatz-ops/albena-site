# Security

## Passkeys

Passkeys replace passwords. They're more secure because they're stored on your device and never sent over the internet.

### Add a passkey

1. Go to [account.albena.ai](https://account.albena.ai)
2. Click **Settings** > **Security**
3. Click **Add passkey**
4. Complete the biometric or PIN verification on your device

You can add multiple passkeys across different devices.

### Remove a passkey

1. Go to **Settings** > **Security**
2. Find the passkey you want to remove
3. Click **Remove**

Keep at least one passkey to avoid being locked out.

## Sessions

A session is an active sign-in to your account on a device.

### View active sessions

1. Go to **Settings** > **Security**
2. Scroll to **Active sessions**
3. See all devices currently signed in to your account

### Sign out everywhere

1. Go to **Settings** > **Security**
2. Click **Sign out everywhere**
3. All sessions end immediately
4. You'll need to sign in again on any device you use

This is useful if you think your account has been compromised.

## Remote access

By default, remote access is **off**. This means Albena can only be accessed from devices on your local network.

### Enable remote access

If you need to access Albena from outside your home network:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Hub**
2. Click your Hub name
3. Turn on **Remote access**

Remote access is encrypted end-to-end. Only enable it if you need it.

## Tips for staying secure

- Use a unique email address for your Albena account
- Add multiple passkeys on different devices
- Keep your devices updated
- Sign out of the portal if you're using a shared computer
- Contact us immediately if you think your account has been compromised
