# Household

## Add household members

Invite family members to share your Albena account.

1. Go to [account.albena.ai](https://account.albena.ai) and open **Household**
2. Enter their email address
3. Click **Send invitation**

They receive an email. When they sign in at account.albena.ai with that same email address, they join your household as a member. The invitation is good for 14 days, and you can cancel it from the same page until it is used.

Someone who already runs an Albena account of their own (with a plan, a Hub or an order) keeps it, and the invitation stays pending. They would need to use a different email address for now.

## Roles

**Owner**  
Full access. Can manage billing, household members, security settings, and Hub configuration.

**Member**  
Can use Albena and see household information, but can't change billing or security settings.

## Manage members

In **Household**, the owner can:
- See everyone in the household and any open invitations
- Cancel an invitation
- Remove a member (they lose access immediately)

## Switching accounts

To sign out of one account and sign in to another:

1. Click your profile picture in the top right
2. Click **Sign out**
3. Go to [account.albena.ai](https://account.albena.ai)
4. Sign in with your other account
