# Household

## Add household members

Invite family members to share your Albena Hub and account.

1. Go to [account.albena.ai](https://account.albena.ai)
2. Click **Settings** > **Household**
3. Click **Invite member**
4. Enter their email address
5. Choose their role (see below)
6. Click **Send invite**

They'll receive an email with a link to join your household.

## Roles

**Owner**  
Full access. Can manage billing, household members, security settings, and Hub configuration.

**Member**  
Can use Albena and see household information, but can't change billing or security settings.

## Manage members

In **Settings** > **Household**, you can:
- View all household members
- Change a member's role (Owner only)
- Remove a member from your household (Owner only)

## Switching accounts

To sign out of one account and sign in to another:

1. Click your profile picture in the top right
2. Click **Sign out**
3. Go to [account.albena.ai](https://account.albena.ai)
4. Sign in with your other account
