# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability
Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately:

- GitHub: use "Report a vulnerability" under the repository's Security tab (private advisory), or
- Email: omar@dbaomarhuertasllc.com with subject "albena.ai security".

Include steps to reproduce, affected URL and impact. Do not access other people's data, run denial-of-service tests, or publicly disclose before we respond.

We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 business days and to keep you updated until resolved. We do not currently offer a bug bounty. Good-faith research within these rules will not be pursued legally.

## Scope
albena.ai and its API. Third-party platforms (Cloudflare, GitHub) should be reported to those vendors.

## Status
Controls are being mapped to common frameworks (see `controls/`). albena.ai is not certified or audited against any framework.
