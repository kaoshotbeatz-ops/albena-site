How it works

Local first. You in charge.

Three simple principles shape everything Albena does.

  1. 1

    On-device first

    Everyday requests run on your own hardware with local models. Fast, private, and they keep working when the internet doesn't.

  2. 2

    Cloud only when needed

    For the rare hard question, Albena can reach for a larger model. She tells you when she does, and shares only what's needed.

  3. 3

    You approve every action

    Sending a message, unlocking a door, making a change: Albena proposes, you say yes. Nothing happens behind your back.

Privacy

Your home is not our data. We never sell your data and never use conversations for advertising. Local processing is the default, not an upgrade. You can see everything Albena remembers and delete it any time, and every action that touches the outside world needs your say-so.
