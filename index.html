What she does

One assistant. The whole household.

Albena handles the small things that add up, so you can stay in the moment.

Voice, naturally

Say “Hey Albena” from across the room. She understands real sentences, not rigid commands.

Home & devices

Lights, climate, locks, cameras, and media in one conversation. Ask once and it's done.

Calendar, mail & briefings

A calm daily briefing, drafted replies, and reminders that show up before you need them.

Memory that learns you

She remembers your preferences, routines, and the people in your life, and you can see or erase any of it.

Privacy-first local AI

Your requests are handled on devices you own wherever possible. What happens at home stays at home.

How it works

Local first. You in charge.

Three simple principles shape everything Albena does.

  1. 1

    On-device first

    Everyday requests run on your own hardware with local models. Fast, private, and they work even when the internet doesn't.

  2. 2

    Cloud only when needed

    For the rare hard question, Albena can reach for a larger model. She tells you when she does, and shares only what's needed.

  3. 3

    You approve every action

    Sending a message, unlocking a door, making a change: Albena proposes, you say yes. Nothing happens behind your back.

Our privacy promise

Your home is not our data.

  • We never sell your data, and we never use your conversations to train advertising.
  • Local processing is the default, not an upgrade.
  • You can see what Albena remembers, and delete it any time.
  • Every action that touches the outside world needs your say-so.
