> **DRAFT for Omar's approval. Not in effect until approved, signed and dated.** Owner: Omar Huertas, Omar Huertas LLC. Review: annually. Approved: ____ (date) by ____.

## Purpose
Protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of albena.ai and the data it handles.

## Policy
1. Scope: albena.ai site, Cloudflare Worker, D1 database, GitHub repository, operator workstation, supporting accounts.
2. Roles: the owner (Omar Huertas) is system owner, security officer and approver; delegates must be named in writing.
3. Controls follow NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5 Moderate, tailored in controls/nist-800-53-moderate.csv; SOC 2 and CSF 2.0 mappings are maintained alongside.
4. Risk: perform and record a risk assessment annually and on major change.
5. Awareness: the operator completes security and privacy awareness annually; records kept.
6. Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (provider) is required for all non-public data.
7. Exceptions require a written, time-boxed risk acceptance by the owner.
8. Public statements about compliance require audit evidence (see GAPS.md).

## Compliance and exceptions
Violations are handled by the owner. Exceptions are written, time-limited and recorded in an Obvera Change.
