# Acceptable Use Policy

> **DRAFT for Omar's approval. Not in effect until approved, signed and dated.** Owner: Omar Huertas, Omar Huertas LLC. Review: annually. Approved: ____ (date) by ____.

## Purpose
Rules for people using systems and data that support albena.ai.

## Policy
1. Use only approved, updated devices with full-disk encryption, screen lock (5 minutes max) and anti-malware where available.
2. Use MFA and a password manager; never share credentials or paste secrets into chats, tickets or code.
3. Do not install unapproved software on the operator workstation used for admin.
4. Use only approved services for storing company data; no personal accounts.
5. Do not attempt to bypass controls; test only systems you own or are authorized to test.
6. Contractors sign an access agreement before receiving access and are offboarded on completion.
7. Report lost devices or suspected compromise immediately; violations may lead to access removal or contract termination.

## Compliance and exceptions
Violations are handled by the owner. Exceptions are written, time-limited and recorded in an Obvera Change.
