## Purpose
Collect the minimum personal data, keep it only as long as needed, and respect individuals' rights.

## Policy
1. Data classes: public content; waitlist emails and support messages (personal, confidential); audit_log (internal, may include IP/user agent).
2. Retention (proposed, confirm): waitlist until removal request or 24 months of inactivity; support messages 24 months; audit_log 12 months; backups 35 days.
3. Delete on request within 30 days and confirm; verify in D1 and backups age-out.
4. Collect only fields shown in the privacy notice; update privacy.html before any new collection.
5. Do not sell personal data. Sharing only with processors listed in the vendor register.
6. Breach affecting personal data triggers the Incident Response Policy and legal notification analysis.
7. Disposal of media follows provider processes; workstation disks are encrypted and wiped before disposal.

## Compliance and exceptions
Violations are handled by the owner. Exceptions are written, time-limited and recorded in an Obvera Change.
