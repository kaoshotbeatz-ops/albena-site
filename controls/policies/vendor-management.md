## Purpose
Understand and control risk from third parties that process our data or run our service.

## Policy
1. Maintain a vendor register: Cloudflare (hosting, DNS, Access, Turnstile, D1), GitHub (source, CI), identity provider, registrar, email provider.
2. Before adoption and annually: obtain current SOC 2 Type II (or ISO 27001), review exceptions and complementary user entity controls, and record the review date.
3. Review terms for data processing, breach notification and sub-processors; sign DPAs where personal data is processed.
4. Prefer vendors with published security programs; document the exit plan for critical vendors.
5. Restrict vendor access to the minimum; remove unused integrations and tokens.

## Compliance and exceptions
Violations are handled by the owner. Exceptions are written, time-limited and recorded in an Obvera Change.
